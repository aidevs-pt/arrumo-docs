Gemini makes thumbnails and images from your style references. Use it alongside [OpenAI](../openai/images.mdx) or [Codex](../codex/setup.mdx#make-images), and put it first when you prefer its look.

When a format asks for thumbnails, the agent writes one image prompt per thumbnail and Arrumo asks the image providers in the video's order. Generated thumbnails are saved as workspace assets and wait for your review with the titles and descriptions. You can also pick Gemini under **Made with** in **Assets › Generate**.

## Set it up

Create a key in [Google AI Studio](https://aistudio.google.com/apikey) and paste it under Gemini in **Settings › Services and keys**, for every workspace or for one. Under **Which plugin does what, in order**, move Gemini up for **Image generation** to prefer it. It's skipped when it has no key.

## Sizes and references

Gemini makes images in 16:9, 9:16, 1:1, 4:3 and 3:4, and follows up to three reference images of up to 20 MB each.
