Beyond narration and transcription, the ElevenLabs plugin makes music and sound effects for your videos, and works on the sound of clips you already have. Everything uses the key from [Narrate videos with ElevenLabs](narration.mdx) and your ElevenLabs credits. Music, dubbing and some models need a paid ElevenLabs plan; when yours lacks one, Arrumo shows ElevenLabs' answer as a plain message.

## Music and sound effects

Open **Assets › Generate** and choose **Music** or **Sound effect**. Describe it and choose its length; the result joins the workspace's assets. The agent can ask for them too, for a video's background or a transition.

| Kind          | Length                     | Example                                                                   |
| ------------- | -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Music         | 3 seconds to 10 minutes    | Calm lo-fi beat with soft piano, no vocals, steady tempo for a tutorial.  |
| Sound effect  | Half a second to 30 seconds | A crisp whoosh as a card slides in.                                      |

Music can take a minute or two.

## Change, isolate or dub a voice

Select an audio or video asset in **Assets**. Each of these makes a new asset and leaves the original as it is.

<Tabs items={["Change voice", "Isolate voice", "Dub"]}>
<Tab value="Change voice">

Says the same words again in another voice, keeping the timing and delivery, with ElevenLabs' speech-to-speech model. Enter a **Voice ID**, or leave it empty to use the voice from the plugin's settings. Up to 5 minutes and 50 MB.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Isolate voice">

Keeps the voice and removes background noise and music. From 4.6 seconds up to 1 hour and 500 MB.

</Tab>
<Tab value="Dub">

Translates the speech into another language, in voices like the original. Choose the **Language** and wait a few minutes. Up to 1 hour and 500 MB.

Languages: Arabic, Bulgarian, Chinese, Croatian, Czech, Danish, Dutch, English, Filipino, Finnish, French, German, Greek, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Malay, Polish, Portuguese, Romanian, Russian, Slovak, Spanish, Swedish, Tamil, Turkish and Ukrainian.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

To dub a finished video with every format and its subtitles, see [Auto-Dubbing](../auto-dubbing/dub-a-video.mdx).

## Not covered

Voice design, voice cloning, text to dialogue and forced alignment aren't part of the plugin yet. Make a voice in ElevenLabs and use its ID in Arrumo.
