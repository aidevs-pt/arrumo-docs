Arrumo AI Video Editor is a free, open-source desktop video editor where an AI agent drafts the script, cuts your footage, makes animations and renders every format, and you review each step.

[![Watch “Meet Arrumo” on YouTube, a video made entirely in Arrumo](/tools/arrumo/static/media/videos/01-meet-arrumo.webp)](https://youtu.be/v2KcwE8xuso)

_Every video in these docs was made entirely in Arrumo: the agent wrote, designed, voiced and cut it, and a person approved each step._

- [Use Arrumo](user/index.md): set up a workspace and make your first video, or follow the [tutorial](user/tutorial.md).
- [Build on Arrumo](developer/index.md): connect through the CLI, MCP or A2A, and write plugins.
- [Official plugins](plugins/index.md): the agents and media providers Arrumo ships with.

Every page is also available as Markdown for AI agents: see `llms.txt` and `llms-full.txt` at the root of each language.
