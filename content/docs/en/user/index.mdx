Arrumo is a desktop video editor where an AI agent does the editing and you approve each step. You describe a video, or bring your footage, and the agent writes the script, designs animations in your brand, voices and cuts it, and renders every format you publish. Nothing moves on until you approve it or ask for changes.

## How Arrumo is organized

- A **workspace** is one brand or channel: its context, colors, fonts, logos, assets, animations and outputs. Make one per brand you publish for.
- A **profile** is a kind of video inside a workspace, such as weekly explainers or client showcases, with its own guidance and outputs.
- A **video** starts as a draft: a brief, your footage or both. When you generate it, the agent works through stages (script, assets and animations, previews, titles and thumbnails, final render) and waits for your review at each one.
- **Plugins** add the agent and the services that make media: transcription, voices, music, images and video clips. See [Official plugins](../plugins/index.mdx).

Settings follow the same layers: the app's defaults, then the workspace, then the profile, then the video. The more specific layer wins for settings, while context and comments from every layer add up.
