**Animations** holds overlays and transitions the agent can reuse in any video. Each one has a looping preview, a thumbnail sheet of six moments and a note on when to use it. Building a few before your first video gives every video the same opening, closing and labels.

## Generate an animation

![The Animations page: lower thirds, logo stings and end cards, each with a looping preview](/tools/arrumo/static/media/app/animations-library.webp)

Open **Animations** in the sidebar and choose **Generate with agent**. Under **What should it show?**, describe the piece in plain words: what appears, which brand colors and logos, the timing and where it will be used. The agent designs it with your brand colors, fonts and brand assets, and Arrumo renders the preview. It can take a few minutes.

Three pieces most workspaces need, with requests that worked well:

- **Logo sting**: "About 3 seconds, to open or close a video: on an Ink to Charcoal gradient, the mark rises in with a soft shadow, then the wordmark appears beside it as a white rounded card. Calm, generous space, no other text."
- **End card**: "About 6 seconds on a Paper background: the mark at the top, a heading-font headline, one short body line and the URL wordmark as the call to action. Staggered, gentle entrances, centred."
- **Lower third** (a style template): "About 5 seconds, to name a topic or a speaker: a Charcoal rounded card with a soft shadow sliding in from the left near the bottom-left safe area, a white heading line and a Mist body line inside it. No sound."

Name colors as your **Brand** page names them, and name logos as the brand assets are called. The agent places those files as they are.

## Check and change it

![An animation’s details: its looping preview and when to use it](/tools/arrumo/static/media/app/animations-detail.webp)

Open an item to play its preview and read its usage note. When something is off, choose **Change with agent** and say only what should change, such as "hold the end card one second longer" or "make the wordmark card smaller". The agent updates the item in place and keeps what it was in **Versions**: open a version to compare it, and choose **Bring back version** to restore it. **Duplicate item** copies an item, for example to edit its usage note for another purpose.

To clear out several items at once, choose **Select**, pick them (or **Select all**) and choose **Delete**, then type the sentence shown to confirm. Their media stays in **Assets**, and videos that already used them keep them.

### Templates

An item whose words change from video to video, such as a lower third or a chapter card, can be a template. Its panel lists **What each video fills in**: each input with an example, its limit and what to do and avoid. When a video uses it, the agent fills in the inputs and Arrumo renders that use with its own preview, while the library keeps the original. A video's review shows each use with **Filled in for this use**.

## Canvas layers and components

With the **Canvas layers** plugin on, the agent can also draw parts of an animation as small web pages: 3D scenes, charts that build from your numbers, particles, maps of routes, Lottie animations and shader effects. Arrumo draws them frame by frame in an isolated browser with no network, no files and no access to the rest of Arrumo, in your brand colors and fonts. The [Canvas layers plugin](../plugins/canvas/canvas-layers.mdx) explains the one-time download.

A **component** is a building block for these layers that later animations import, such as a confetti burst or a light sweep in your colors. Choose **Generate with agent**, pick **Reusable component** under **What to make**, and describe what it should do and which options it should have. Arrumo renders a demo. A component's panel shows its demo, its **Import path**, its **Options** and **View code** (read-only). To change it, use **Change with agent**: animations made earlier keep the version they were made with. **Show** filters the library to **Animations and transitions** or **Components**.

## Who can use it

**Visibility** filters the library by the whole workspace or one profile. Items made for the workspace are available to every profile. To give a profile's item to others, use **Share with profiles**.

## In videos

![Reviewing a video’s animations: each one with a preview, a decision and “Save to the library when approved”](/tools/arrumo/static/media/app/review-animations.webp)

During a video's Assets step, the agent places library items that fit the script as they are, such as the sting to open and the end card to close. For anything with words that change from video to video, such as lower thirds, it designs new animations and follows the layout, colors, fonts and timing of your library pieces that do the same job.

So keep fixed text in library items, and treat pieces such as a lower third as style templates: say in the usage note that their words are placeholders.

## With an agent

Agents can do the same through MCP, the command line or A2A: `generate_motion_library_item` takes the request (and `basedOn` to change an existing item), and `list_motion_library`, `duplicate_motion_library_item` and `set_motion_library_shares` manage the library. `list_motion_library_versions` and `restore_motion_library_version` handle versions, `get_motion_template_inputs` reads a template's inputs, and `generate_canvas_component` makes a component. `get_canvas_renderer`, `install_canvas_renderer` and `remove_canvas_renderer` manage the canvas download. The [motion reference](../developer/reference/motion.mdx) describes the layers, shapes and fonts an animation can use.
