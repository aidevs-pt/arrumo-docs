By the end of this tutorial you have a workspace built from your channel: an **About you** the agent drafted from your public pages, your brand colors and avatar, your thumbnails as style references, a profile for each kind of video you make, an output for each platform you post on, and your existing videos in **Assets**, ready to reuse. It takes about twenty minutes.

You need Arrumo and an agent, [Claude Code](../../plugins/claude-code/setup.mdx) or [Codex](../../plugins/codex/setup.mdx), installed and signed in.

<Callout title="What Arrumo reads, and what it doesn't">
  Arrumo opens each link on your computer the way a visitor who isn't signed in would, keeps the page's name, description, visible text, images and theme color, and gives that to your agent to draft from. It never signs in to your accounts, and it doesn't download your videos or posts, or read your analytics, comments or private content. Nothing is saved until you press **Save**.
</Callout>

## What each platform shows

Platforms decide what a visitor who isn't signed in can see, so some links give the agent much more to work with than others. This is what Arrumo found on each when we tested it in November 2026; platforms change their pages, so treat it as a guide.

| Platform                               | Link to add                          | What Arrumo gets                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| YouTube                                | `youtube.com/@yourchannel`           | Channel name, description and avatar, plus the thumbnails of the videos on the page.               |
| YouTube video                          | `youtube.com/watch?v=…`              | The video's title and full description: a good sample of how you write.                             |
| Instagram                              | `instagram.com/yourname`             | Name, follower and post counts, the start of your bio and your profile picture.                     |
| TikTok                                 | `tiktok.com/@yourname`               | Often only the page title. Add your bio in the notes, or link your website or link-in-bio page.    |
| X, Threads, Bluesky                    | your profile                         | Name, bio and profile picture.                                                                      |
| LinkedIn, Facebook, Twitch             | your page                            | Name, description and picture.                                                                      |
| Your website or link-in-bio page       | the home or about page               | Usually the richest source: who you are, what you offer, your logo and your colors.                |

<Callout type="info" title="Pick the links that describe you">
  You can add up to eight. A good set is your main channel, two or three of your best videos, your other profiles and your website. Arrumo reads only the page at each address, not the pages it links to.
</Callout>

<Steps>
<Step>

### Create the workspace and add your links

On first launch, Arrumo asks for a workspace name, the agent to use and **Links to read**. Paste your channel first, then the rest, and choose **Create … and read the links** (it names your workspace).

For a workspace you already have, open **Workspace settings › Context**. Under **Where can Claude Code learn about you?** (it names your agent), paste a link in **Links**, use **Add another link** for each of the others, and choose **Draft my context**.

![Workspace settings, Context: the links the agent read and the About you it drafted](/tools/arrumo/static/media/app/context.webp)

Use **Anything else to know** for what your pages don't say: what you make, who it's for, how you like to sound, and, for TikTok, your bio. Reading and drafting takes about a minute.

</Step>
<Step>

### Review About you

The agent writes **About you** under the same headings for every creator: **Who you are**, **What you make**, **Audience**, **Voice and tone**, **Visual style**, **Do and don't** and **Links**. It fills in only what your pages and notes support, and leaves a short placeholder in [brackets] where it couldn't tell.

Read it like a brief for a new editor. Replace every placeholder, correct anything it guessed, and add what every video should respect, such as how you open videos, words you never use or the call to action you always end with. Then **Save**. Every script, cut and title starts from this page.

</Step>
<Step>

### Keep what it found on your pages

Under **Also found on your pages**, the agent offers:

- **Brand colors** it could tell from your pages, such as the theme color and your avatar's accents.
- **Images**, already named, grouped and described: your avatar and logos as brand assets, your video thumbnails as thumbnail references with notes on their style, so new thumbnails match it, and other useful pictures as assets.

Untick anything you don't want and choose **Add to workspace** (the button counts what's ticked). Arrumo downloads the images into the workspace and tells you if any couldn't be downloaded.

If you publish distinct kinds of videos, such as a weekly series and short tips, the agent also lists **Suggested profiles**, each with editing guidance and a color. Choose **Create profile** on the ones that fit.

</Step>
<Step>

### Check your brand

Open **Workspace settings › Brand**. Name each color and tick the ones videos may use; avatars rarely carry a full palette, so add any missing colors by hand. Add your heading and body fonts, Regular and Bold, so animations use them. See [Set up your brand](../brand.mdx) for every option.

</Step>
<Step>

### Add an output for each platform

Open **Workspace settings › Outputs**. Every workspace starts with **Landscape** (1920 × 1080) and **Portrait** (1080 × 1920):

- **Landscape** for YouTube videos.
- **Portrait** for Shorts, TikTok and Reels. On **Landscape**, ask for **Portrait cuts** to also get short vertical clips taken from each approved video.
- **Add format** for anything else, such as a square post.

On each format, ask for titles, descriptions, tags and thumbnails, and put the platform's rules under **Additional specifications**, such as "titles under 60 characters" or "end every description with a link to the newsletter".

![The Landscape output with thumbnails and reference images](/tools/arrumo/static/media/app/outputs.webp)

</Step>
<Step>

### Bring in your existing videos

Arrumo doesn't download videos from platforms. To reuse your own videos, as footage for new ones or for portrait cuts, download the originals from each platform's own tools: YouTube Studio lets you download the videos you uploaded, and TikTok and Instagram include your videos when you download your account data from their settings. Your original recordings, if you still have them, are better still.

Then open **Assets** and choose **Upload**. Describe each video in its notes so the agent knows what's in it. To make a video from them, add them to a new draft from **Videos › Drafts**, as in [Create and export your first video](../first-video.mdx).

</Step>
<Step>

### Read your links again later

**Context** keeps the links it read and shows **Read** or **Could not be read** for each. When your channel changes, open it again: it shows when they were last read and lets you read them again or add more. Forget a link you no longer want with the button beside it.

</Step>
</Steps>

## When a link can't be read

<Accordions>
  <Accordion title="None of the links could be read">
    Check each address in a private browser window. Arrumo reads only public pages over the internet, never addresses on your own network. If your profiles are private or behind a sign-in, add a few words about yourself in **Anything else to know** and draft from those, or link your website instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A link shows Could not be read">
    The page may need you to be signed in, may be private, may not exist or may have taken too long to answer. Some platforms show a consent or region page first. Try the profile's plain address (`instagram.com/yourname` rather than a share link), or replace it with another page that describes you.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The draft is thin or generic">
    The pages had little text, as TikTok profiles often do. Add links to a few of your videos, your website or your link-in-bio page, write a few lines in **Anything else to know**, and draft again. You can also edit **About you** by hand at any time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Thumbnails weren't found">
    Only YouTube lists video thumbnails on the channel page. For other platforms, save a few of your thumbnails or covers and upload them under the format's **Thumbnail reference images** in **Outputs**.
  </Accordion>
</Accordions>

## With an agent

Everything here is also available to agents over [MCP, the CLI and A2A](../../developer/connect.mdx):

| Tool                       | What it does                                                                                                       |
| -------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| `draft_workspace_context`  | Reads up to eight public pages and returns a draft of the context, profiles, colors and images, without saving it. |
| `set_workspace_context`    | Saves the context Markdown.                                                                                        |
| `import_workspace_context` | Adds the chosen colors, images and profiles from a draft to the workspace.                                         |
| `list_context_sources`     | Lists the links read, whether each could be read, and when.                                                        |
| `forget_context_source`    | Forgets one link.                                                                                                  |
